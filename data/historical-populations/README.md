# Historical population data

Population records must have a traceable source. Do not treat provisional gameplay values as historical observations.

Proposed fields: settlement_id, name_modern, name_historical, historical_territory, reference_year, population, data_type (census/estimate/interpolation), source_title, source_url, source_page, confidence, notes.

Starting research leads (verify dataset licensing and accessibility before importing):
- Buringh European urban population dataset: https://doi.org/10.17026/dans-xzy-u62q
- De Vries historical European urbanization datasets
- Bairoch, Batou and Chèvre historical city populations
- Vision of Britain historical census: https://www.visionofbritain.org.uk/census/index.jsp
- ifo Prussian Economic History Database: https://www.ifo.de/en/welcome-ipehd-ifo-prussian-economic-history-database

The year 1800 is not a Europe-wide census year. Preserve the observation year and whether a value is estimated.
